01 — Profile

Researching security at the intersection of strategy, technology and proliferation.

I am an early-career researcher and Master's candidate specialising in international security and strategic studies, with a particular interest in nuclear strategy, arms control and non-proliferation.

My research also examines authoritarian strategic behaviour, illicit WMD and CBRN trafficking, dual-use technologies, space security and the political dimensions of contemporary security threats.

My work has been published through international editorial collaborations involving The Sundial Press at Sciences Po and The Politic at Yale University.
